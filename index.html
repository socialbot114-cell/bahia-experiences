FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I pay, and in what currency?

Prices are shown in USD per person, and the final rate for each experience is confirmed before booking. In Brazil you can pay by credit card or PIX, and we can help with currency exchange.

Will my guide speak English?

Yes. Our local guides can assist in English and Portuguese. Ask us to confirm the language for your date when you book.

Can I book a private transfer?

Yes. Private transfers can be arranged between selected stops. Pickup points and availability depend on the experience you choose.

How do Pataxó cultural visits work?

A cultural visit can be included when it is arranged with local community hosts. We confirm the visit details with them before you book.
